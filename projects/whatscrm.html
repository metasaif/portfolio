PROJECT AT A GLANCE

WhatsCRM: the short version.

A WhatsApp-focused CRM interface study exploring contacts, conversations and lead follow-ups. View the proposed workflow and project notes.

Intended audience
Teams organizing inquiries, conversations and follow-ups.
Design direction
The pipeline groups sample leads by their next step: new inquiry, conversation and follow-up. It illustrates a workflow; it does not demonstrate a live WhatsApp integration.
Evidence available
Written concept and illustrative interface. No production release, customer results or verified implementation stack is asserted.
