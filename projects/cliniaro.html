Cliniaro: the short version.

A clinic workflow study connecting appointments, patient context, prescriptions and billing. Explore the interface direction and its documented limits.

Intended audience
Clinic teams coordinating appointments and patient information.
Design direction
The schedule groups appointment times and patient context in one view. The appointment-to-prescription map explains the intended sequence without presenting it as a verified clinical system.
Evidence available
Written concept and illustrative interface. No production release, customer results or verified implementation stack is asserted.

01 / PROBLEM & CONTEXT

Where it starts.

Clinic work crosses many separate touchpoints: scheduling, patient information, prescriptions and billing. The product direction is to bring those workflows into one coherent workspace.

02 / SOLUTION & INTERFACE

A connected workspace.

A shared clinic workspace, organized around the patient journey. The interface study below explores how a daily schedule and patient context could sit together.
